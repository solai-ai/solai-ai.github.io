Solana AI Infrastructure

SOLAI is a decentralized AI infrastructure ecosystem combining AI Agents (SOLAI Coder), Exclusive LLM Models, and DePIN Compute — enabling users to share, rent, and monetize AI resources through the SOLAI Network.

SOLAI Network turns local models, idle GPUs, provider marketplaces and temporary AI clusters into one programmable compute layer for the agent economy.

The Problem

AI demand is scaling faster than centralized infrastructure can comfortably absorb.

Startups, agents and builders need reliable inference without depending entirely on hyperscaler capacity, opaque pricing or single-provider availability.

01

Compute concentration

High-performance AI infrastructure is concentrated in a small number of capital-intensive cloud platforms, creating pricing and availability dependency.

02

Energy bottlenecks

IEA projects data center electricity consumption to double by 2030, while AI-focused facilities grow faster than conventional workloads.

03

Idle hardware

Powerful GPUs in workstations, labs, servers and gaming rigs often sit underused. SOLAI makes that capacity discoverable and rentable.

Architecture

Local AI first. Network compute when the task outgrows the machine.

Users can run models locally through Ollama, then switch to decentralized providers or agent-assembled clusters when they need more capacity.

solai chat
solai provider enable
solai compute list --model SOLAI-80B
solai compute rent 291
solai coder "Analyze 50,000 documents"
How It Works

Distributed hardware becomes a temporary AI cluster.

SOLAI Coder decomposes the task, selects models and providers, manages escrow budgets, retries failed jobs and aggregates validated results.

1

Task

User or agent defines budget, deadline and quality.

2

Plan

SOLAI Coder creates a task graph.

3

Rent

Providers are selected through marketplace criteria.

4

Execute

Jobs run off-chain through Ollama-compatible runtimes.

5

Settle

Escrow pays providers after validation.
